# Motion spec

- Entering test mode: the selected star leaves the constellation and travels to the machine along an arc while its specimen grows from nothing; the camera moves to the machine. Leaving reverses it.
- Tensile: the crosshead rises with the specimen's stretch; the gauge narrows around a neck and, at fracture, opens a gap and tapers the broken ends. Bending: the bar takes the three-point deflection shape and, when brittle, splits at midspan. Impact: the pendulum swings down, strikes, and rises by the angle the specimen left it.
- The needle follows the load; the machine's display and the panel's chart grow from the same curve at the same time.
- Changing a weight moves every star with a spring toward its new place; changing the selected material morphs the specimen's colour, metalness and roughness instead of swapping it.
- Ambient motion (star pulse, hero rotation, ply breathing) stops under Pause Motion and reduced motion.
