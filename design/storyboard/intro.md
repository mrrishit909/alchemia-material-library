# Intro storyboard (about 22 s, skippable at any time)

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0.6 s | A dark lab. A sphere of amorphous metal, titanium grey. |
| 1.8 to 5.4 s | The temperature rises: it glows copper, orange, white-hot; it softens, droops and runs. |
| 5.6 to 7.8 s | It cools and pulls into crystals. |
| 7.8 to 13.4 s | Metal grains, a carbon lattice, ceramic microstructure, composite layers: the surface morphs through each. |
| 13.4 to 17 s | Property labels flash one by one with their real range across the library: strength, thermal conductivity, density, fatigue, cost, carbon. |
| 17 to 20 s | The sphere divides: its 44 stars fly from its surface to their places in the constellation; the sphere shrinks away. |
| 19 to 21.6 s | The interface wipes in around the constellation. |
